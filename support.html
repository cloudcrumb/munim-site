Help with Munim, and how to reach a human.

Munim has a full guide built in. Open the app and tap ? on the home screen, or go to More → Help & Guide. It is searchable in plain language — type "maid", "80G", "backup" or "new phone".

Common questions

  • Do I need an account or internet? No. Munim has no login and no server, and works fully offline.
  • I got a new phone. On the old phone, More → Export Backup and save the file somewhere safe. On the new one, install Munim and use More → Restore Backup.
  • How do I back up? More → Export Backup writes a single file and opens the share sheet. Do it once a month, or after a big batch of entries. Because nothing is stored on a server, backups are yours to keep.
  • My balance looks wrong. A balance is the opening balance plus everything recorded against that account. Check for an entry saved against the wrong payment source, a payment never recorded, or an opening balance never set. The Statement screen is the best place to trace it line by line.
  • How is my maid's salary calculated? Open her page and tap the pending month. The settle screen shows the full working — each job, absences, paid leave, deductions, hours, extras and any advance recovered.

Reporting a problem

Email what happened, which screen you were on, and what you expected instead. If an amount looks wrong, the date and the figures you were expecting help a great deal. Please do not send your backup file unless asked — it contains your household's entire finances.
