Munim

Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

Munim collects nothing. There is no account, no login and no server. Everything you record stays in a database file on your own device, and the app works with no internet connection at all.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, transmit, store or have access to any information about you or your household. We could not look at your data if we wanted to — there is nowhere for it to go. Munim has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no third-party tracking libraries of any kind.

Where your data lives

Every entry you make — expenses, income, staff records, attendance, salaries, bills, loans, donations, insurance policies, bank balances and settings — is written to a private SQLite database inside Munim's own storage on your phone. iOS prevents other apps from reading it. Deleting Munim deletes that database and everything in it.

Permissions, and why

  • Face ID / Touch ID — only to unlock Munim when you switch App Lock on. The check happens entirely on your device; Munim never receives your biometric data, and your phone's own passcode is never used or seen.
  • Photo library and camera — only when you choose a photo as the icon for a staff member, bank account or contact. The image is copied into Munim's local storage. Your library is not scanned or uploaded.
  • Notifications — only for the bill and attendance reminders you turn on. They are scheduled locally by your phone. There is no push server, so no reminder ever travels over a network.

Backups you choose to export

Munim can export your whole database as a single backup file. This only happens when you tap Export Backup, and the file is handed to the iOS share sheet so you can decide where it goes — Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email, anywhere. Once you send it somewhere, that copy is governed by whichever service you chose, not by us. We never see it.

Children

Munim is rated 4+ and is safe for any age, because it collects no information from anyone.

Selling or sharing data

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose personal information, because we never have any. There are no advertising partners, data brokers or analytics vendors involved in this app.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Munim ever handles data differently, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
