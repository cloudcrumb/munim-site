Munim · मुनीम

The family munim, on your phone and nowhere else.

A household ledger for Indian homes — daily spending, the maid's salary and attendance, recurring bills, udhaar, donations, and a printable cash-and-bank statement. No login, no server, no ads.

What it does

  • Everyday spending in three taps, cash or UPI, with a category breakdown that shows where the month actually went.
  • Staff — cooks, drivers, house help, gardeners. Many jobs per person, paid monthly, per visit or by the hour. Attendance where you mark only the exceptions, advances recovered across months, and a month-end receipt showing the whole working.
  • Bills and premiums with reminders before and on the due day, and a single tap to record a payment and roll the cycle forward.
  • Loans and donations — who owes whom, and an 80G total kept ready for tax time.
  • A real statement for any period: opening balance, receipts, payments, closing balance, split into cash and bank. Exportable as a PDF.

Privacy is the whole design

Munim has no account and no backend. Your household's finances live in a database file on your own phone, locked behind Face ID if you want, and nothing is ever transmitted anywhere. The only copy that exists elsewhere is a backup you export yourself.
